Type an address or keywords, then press Enter.

  • how to bake a cake is searched on Google.
  • node.js and homework.pdf are searched on Google too, because their endings are not domain names.

Tabs

  • Middle-click (or Ctrl+click) a link to open it in a background tab. A background tab only loads when you open it.
  • Middle-click a tab's title to close it.
  • A faded tab title means the tab is asleep. Tabs not seen for 5 minutes are put to sleep to save memory, except WhatsApp Web, tabs playing sound, and tabs with something typed in them. When the laptop is almost out of memory, the other tabs are put to sleep right away.
  • Tabs that are still open when the browser is closed come back the next time it is opened, even after the laptop shuts down suddenly. Those tabs sleep until opened, so they use no memory.
  • Closed one by accident? Press Ctrl+Shift+T to reopen it, with its back/forward history.

Bookmarks

  • Press Ctrl+D or the star button next to the address bar to save a page. Pressing a filled star removes it.
  • Bookmarks appear as shortcut boxes on the home page, below the search box.
  • Bookmarks can be renamed and removed on the Bookmarks page.

Learn

  • The Learn page in the top menu is a notebook for school. Notes are saved as ordinary Markdown files in the ~/kevin-catatan folder, one folder per subject, so they open without an internet connection and in any text editor.
  • Press Write a note to create a new note, or Edit on a note to change it. The format is Markdown: # Heading, - list, **bold**, and [[note-name]] to link another note.
  • Each subject has a card with its latest notes. Click the subject's name to see all of its notes. The search box at the top right searches all notes.
  • Turn material into notes: AI turns a PDF, a text file, or pasted text into notes, one per topic. It uses your own DeepSeek API key, set on the AI assistant page. The estimated cost is shown before anything is sent, and the same document is never processed twice.
  • Ask AI: learn by chatting with the AI, as with a tutor. It looks in the notes first, explains a little at a time with examples, then asks a question back to check understanding. Write your reply in the box at the bottom and press Send. The AI names the notes it used and marks explanations that don't come from them. For homework it is told to help step by step rather than hand over the answer. Ask about this note on each note starts a chat about that note. The cost of each answer is shown under it, and conversations aren't saved.

Chess

  • The Chess page in the top menu replays your games on a board of its own. Connect a Lichess and/or Chess.com account with just the username: games there are public, so no login or password is needed.
  • Press Get latest games to fetch your latest 30 games from Lichess and this month's archive from Chess.com. Games are kept on the laptop, so the list is still there without internet. Filter by result, color, or site, or search for an opponent or opening.
  • On the board, use the buttons under the move list or the left and right arrow keys; Home and End jump to the start and the end. Games analysed on Lichess show an evaluation bar and the ?, ??, and ?! marks.
  • Paste PGN opens a game from anywhere, also from a Lichess game link. The notation can be loose, for example 0-0 or e2e4.
  • Save as a note puts the game in Learn › Catur, with room to write down its lessons.
  • Variants such as Chess960 aren't supported yet.

History and data

  • Every web page you open is recorded in the history, for up to 90 days. The history can be searched, and each visit can be removed with the × button.
  • Logins stay saved even when the browser is closed.
  • Settings shows how much data is stored, with buttons to clear the history, cache, cookies, or site data, from the last hour up to all time.

Ads and trackers

  • Ads and trackers are blocked automatically. The lists are downloaded the first time the browser is opened, then updated once a week.
  • Tracking parameters in addresses, such as utm_source, fbclid, and si in YouTube links, are removed.
  • The blocker's status is shown in Settings.

YouTube

  • YouTube opens in its mobile version (m.youtube.com), which uses much less memory. It looks like it does on a tablet. Videos, search, comments, and sign-in are all there, and music keeps playing when you switch to another tab.
  • The video quality is chosen by YouTube, usually 360p, and can be changed in the player's settings. Videos above 720p are reported to sites as unsupported, so the laptop is not overloaded.
  • Want the desktop version? Open www.youtube.com/?app=desktop&persist_app=1. To go back to the light version, open m.youtube.com/?app=m&persist_app=1. YouTube remembers this choice in a cookie; if the cookies are cleared, YouTube goes back to the light version.

Video

Videos on web pages, YouTube included, don't play by themselves. Click a video to play it. Only WhatsApp Web is an exception.

Camera, microphone, and notifications

Only WhatsApp Web may use the camera and microphone, for example for voice messages. Permission is given automatically. While they are on, a camera or microphone icon appears in the tab's title. Video calls don't work yet.

WhatsApp Web may also show notifications for incoming messages while its tab is not visible. Click a notification to go straight to WhatsApp. Other sites are always refused, and so is location access.

Language

Kevin Browser can be used in Indonesian or English. Change it in Settings, under Bahasa · Language. This changes the menus, buttons, messages, and Kevin Browser's own pages. Websites keep their own language.

Pages about Kevin Browser

The menu at the top holds Home, Learn, Chess, and Settings. Story, Pros & Cons, Guide, Plans, and About Us are at the very bottom of every page.

Downloads

Downloaded files go to your Downloads folder. If a name is already taken, a number is added: homework (1).pdf.

Installing on another laptop

Copy the package file kevin-browser_…_amd64.deb, then on the other laptop open a terminal in that file's folder and run:

sudo apt install ./kevin-browser_*_amd64.deb

Everything it needs, including WebKitGTK and the video codecs, is installed along with it. The laptop must run Linux Mint 22 or Ubuntu 24.04 or later, 64-bit.

From a terminal, the browser opens with kevin-browser, or straight to an address: kevin-browser detik.com.

New versions of Kevin Browser arrive through the Update Manager, along with other updates.
