Plans

Kevin Browser is still young. This is what we are thinking about for the next steps.

Principle: stay light

All over the world there are families in the same situation as Kevin's. What they need is not a sophisticated browser, but one that keeps working even on an old, weak laptop. That is why this project was born. So future development won't chase sophisticated features; it will focus on making Kevin Browser work well on laptops with limited power. The plans below will only be built if they stay light.

Agentic features

This is the age of AI. The plan is for Kevin Browser to have an AI assistant you can work with inside the browser, not one that only answers questions: one that turns study material into notes, then helps you learn from them. The first step is already here: the notebook on the Learn page.

Chess is our children's hobby. There will be something related to chess. What form it takes is still being thought through.
