Kevin Browser

A lightweight browser for learning, on a simple laptop.

Light on memory

Opening google.com takes just 408 MB, including every process behind it.

Sleeping tabs

Only 2–4 tabs stay awake at once, and tabs left unseen for 5 minutes go to sleep. They wake up when opened, even after the browser is closed and opened again.

No ads

Ads and trackers are blocked. detik.com takes 497 MB, not 1150 MB.

One file

The whole program is a single 4.9 MB file. It can be shared on a flash drive.

Kevin Browser was born from a second-hand laptop with very little RAM. Its goal is simple: to keep opening the modern web for learning, both school lessons and chess, without using up the memory. The Chess page replays your games from Lichess and Chess.com; more chess features are being planned.
