Why this browser exists

Kevin Browser is our family project. Kevin is my son's name. When this browser was made, in 2026, he was in his first year of senior high school.

Kevin needed a laptop, but we didn't have the money. In the end we bought a very cheap, old second-hand laptop, and installed Linux on it to keep it light.

Linux was indeed light. The problem was the browser: most browsers today are hungry for memory.

That is where Kevin came up with the idea of making his own browser. And so Kevin Browser was born: a browser that can still open the modern web, but is made as light as possible for laptops like Kevin's.

If you also use an old laptop, this browser is for you too.
