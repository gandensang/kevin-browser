About us

Kevin Browser is a family project: made for Kevin and his second-hand laptop, and for anyone who uses a simple laptop.

Built with

  • WebKitGTK: WebKit for Linux, the web engine also used by Safari.
  • GTK 4: windows, buttons, and tabs.
  • C# and .NET: the programming language, compiled into a single standalone file.
  • GirCore: the bridge between C# and GTK and WebKitGTK.
  • EasyList, EasyPrivacy, and ABPindo: the ad and tracker lists used by the blocker, maintained by volunteers.

Thank you to everyone behind those open-source projects.

Source code

Kevin Browser itself is open source too, under the MIT license. Anyone can inspect the code, use it, or help develop it: github.com/gandensang/kevin-browser.
